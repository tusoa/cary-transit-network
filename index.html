When Demand Outruns Capacity: Cary and Its Neighbors

Cary, Apex, and Morrisville are adding residents and riders faster than the transit network can add service. This map shows who funds, governs, operates, and fills gaps around GoCary, and whether those ties run on formal accountability or informal trust. Hover or tap an actor to isolate its ties.

Demand is rising

  • Fast population growth in Cary, Apex, and Morrisville
  • GoApex ADA door-to-door trips moved from GoWake Access to GoCary Door to Door on April 18, 2026
  • On-demand shuttles raise expectations for quick, flexible trips

Capacity lags

  • GoCary relies on drivers supplied by its contractor, MV Transportation
  • New service waits on Wake Transit plan cycles and federal grant timelines
  • Town services for Apex and Morrisville run on GoCary's staff and fleet

Who can close the gap

  • Wake County: transit tax dollars and plan priorities
  • FTA and NCDOT: grants, with conditions attached
  • GoCary: contract terms with MVT and service design
  • Private providers: absorb trips public service cannot
